GStreamer (video playback)
==========================

A Windows build of Cerno with the video feature plays videos through
GStreamer. Its libraries are loaded at run time as separate DLLs beside
cerno.exe, its plugins from the gstreamer-1.0 folder. They come from the
official GStreamer 1.28.7 MSVC build, unchanged. The licence texts of each
project are in the gstreamer folder next to this file.

    Component                      License
    GStreamer, gst-plugins-base,   LGPL-2.0-or-later
      -good, -bad, -ugly, gst-libav
    FFmpeg (avcodec, avformat,     LGPL-2.1-or-later (built without
      avutil, avfilter, swscale,     --enable-gpl and --enable-nonfree)
      swresample)
    GLib, GObject, GIO, GModule    LGPL-2.0-or-later
    proxy-libintl                  LGPL-2.0-or-later
    gst-plugins-rs (dav1d plugin)  MIT or Apache-2.0
    dav1d                          BSD-2-Clause
    libvpx                         BSD-3-Clause (+ patent grant, PATENTS)
    orc                            BSD-2-Clause
    libffi                         MIT
    PCRE2                          BSD-3-Clause
    zlib                           zlib
    bzip2                          bzip2 licence (BSD-style)

You may replace these DLLs with other builds that keep a compatible
interface. Cerno then uses the versions you supply.

Corresponding source of GStreamer 1.28.7:

    https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/src/gstreamer/gstreamer-1.28.7.tar.xz
    https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/src/gst-plugins-base/gst-plugins-base-1.28.7.tar.xz
    https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/src/gst-plugins-good/gst-plugins-good-1.28.7.tar.xz
    https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/src/gst-plugins-bad/gst-plugins-bad-1.28.7.tar.xz
    https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/src/gst-plugins-ugly/gst-plugins-ugly-1.28.7.tar.xz
    https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/src/gst-libav/gst-libav-1.28.7.tar.xz

The libraries GStreamer bundles (FFmpeg, GLib and the others above) were
built by GStreamer's cerbero at the tag 1.28.7; the recipes there name each
version and where its source comes from:

    https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/cerbero/-/tree/1.28.7/recipes

On Linux, Cerno uses the distribution's GStreamer. The .deb package
depends on the distribution's GStreamer packages; the AppImage uses the
GStreamer installed on the system and bundles none.
